How It Works

Built around your biology

Most health tools measure you in snapshots. Fero is designed around the 74-day spermatogenic cycle — the only timeframe over which meaningful change in fertility can actually be measured.

🔬

Why 74 days? Spermatogenesis — the process of producing mature sperm — takes 42–74 days from start to finish. A single test tells you where you are today. Fero tracks the full cycle, so you can see real change over time and understand what's actually driving it.

01
Test
At-home semen analysis and hormone panel kits deliver clinical-grade data without a clinic visit. Sperm count, motility, morphology, testosterone, LH, FSH, and more.
02
Track
Daily lifestyle check-ins — sleep, alcohol, stress, diet, exercise — build your continuous fertility picture. Connects with Oura and Whoop if you already use them.
03
Optimise
Your AI coach ranks the highest-impact lifestyle changes by your specific data and the clinical evidence behind each one. Personalised, not generic.
04
Repeat
Re-test at the end of each 74-day cycle. Measure real change. Watch your Fero Score move as your habits take effect — with the data to prove why.
